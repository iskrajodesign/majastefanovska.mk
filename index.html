MAJA STEFANOVSKA · BIOGRAPHY

Maja Stefanovska (b. 1972, Skopje) is a contemporary Macedonian visual artist whose practice spans over three decades of raw canvas exploration, heavy oil pigments, tactile media, and spatial video art.

Following her early training at the Applied Arts High School '2ri Avgust' (1990), she graduated from the Faculty of Fine Arts (FLU) in Skopje in 1996.

Stefanovska's oeuvre focuses on themes of identity, spatial belonging, and non-verbal emotion—shifting fluidly from monumental oil paintings to multimedia installations and international video projects.
